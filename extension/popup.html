How registration and recovery work

USC checkout submits every pending change together. The helper temporarily unschedules other unregistered selections, registers one course bundle at a time, and restores those selections afterward. It never unschedules registered classes or submits a partial course bundle.

The watchlist is checked about every 30 seconds; USC login is checked separately every 5 minutes. Existing SSO is reused when possible. Password, passkey, or Duo prompts require you.
